## Style

The cartographic values (colours, line widths, zoom scale) are adapted from
[CyclOSM CartoCSS](https://github.com/cyclosm/cyclosm-cartocss-style):

- Based on Mapbox OSM-Bright, under the BSD 3-Clause licence.
- Colours in `palette.mss` are based on Hydda, under the Apache 2.0 licence.

The generated layers build on [`@versatiles/style`](https://github.com/versatiles-org/versatiles-style)
(MIT, © yetzt and Michael Kreil). Generated VersaTiles styles are marked CC0.

## Icons

All icons in `public/sprites/` are recoloured from CC0 1.0 sources:

- `symbols/openstreetmap-carto` from CyclOSM.
- `icons/` from `@versatiles/style` (Maki and VersaTiles sets).

The one-way arrow comes from the VersaTiles sprite served at runtime.
